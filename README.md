# Jesus Loves Tyler — free ministry software, public domain

I'm Tyler — a Christian missionary and programmer in North Carolina. Everything I make is **free, and released into the public domain**: Bibles, devotionals, tools, and games, built so the gospel and good useful things reach the ends of the earth with no paywall and no permission needed.

**The whole work lives at [jesuslovestyler.com](https://jesuslovestyler.com/)** — one page linking everything below.

## The flagships

- **[World English Bible](https://github.com/jesuslovestyler/World-English-Bible)** — the complete World English Bible as a free web app: red-letter text, a big idea and section headings for all 1,189 chapters, Listen (read-aloud and a recorded voice), reading plans, a 404-term Bible-words glossary, Doré illustrations, and the whole Bible as an EPUB. Live: https://jesuslovestyler.github.io/World-English-Bible/
- **[Study Bible](https://github.com/jesuslovestyler/Study-Bible)** — a word-by-word interlinear Bible in Greek, Hebrew, and Aramaic. Every original word carries a translation in its verse, a Strong's number, a definition, synonyms, and parsing — all from public-domain sources (Westminster Leningrad Codex, Robinson–Pierpont Byzantine Textform, Strong's 1890, BDB 1906, Thayer 1889), so no one has to redo the tagging ever again. Live: https://jesuslovestyler.github.io/Study-Bible/
- **[WEB Concordance](https://github.com/jesuslovestyler/WEB-concordance)** — a concordance of the World English Bible: look up a word, find everywhere it speaks. Live: https://jesuslovestyler.github.io/WEB-concordance/

## Also here, in their own repos

- **30+ devotional pages** — the Gospel, Assurance, Psalm 23, Psalm 91, Romans 8, the Armor of God, pages for the grieving, the lonely, the anxious, the sick, and the weary caregiver, and more.
- **30+ free tools** — calculators, a unit converter, a QR code generator, a verse image maker, instruments you can play (synthesizer, harp, flute, xylophone), and everyday helpers.
- **30+ free games** — solitaire, mahjong, chess, checkers, Skee-Ball, and more. Play only; nothing to buy.
- **[Support](https://github.com/jesuslovestyler/Support)** — the Support the Work page: everything stays free always; support is voluntary partnership for those who want to help keep it online and growing.
